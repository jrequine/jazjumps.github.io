Sync Equestrian Ambassador

More exciting news! I'm delighted to announce that I've joined Sync Equestrian as an ambassador. If you've been following along, you'll know how much good quality riding kit matters to me—and Sync really delivers.

Because Every Rider is an Athlete

That's Sync's tagline, and I love it. It captures exactly how I feel about our sport. We put in the hours, the training, the dedication—and we deserve kit that performs as hard as we do.

Sync Equestrian focuses on premium equestrian apparel that's designed with the competitive rider in mind. Their breeches are particularly impressive—they offer everything from full seat to knee patch, pull-on styles, and even fleece-lined options for those cold winter mornings at the yard.

What I Love

I've been trying out some of their pieces and I'm really impressed with the quality. The fit is spot on, the materials feel premium, and they look great both in the saddle and around the yard. They also have some lovely themed collections if you like to coordinate your look.

Beyond breeches, they do tops, accessories, belts, gloves, saddle pads—pretty much everything you need. They've even got a young rider collection, which is brilliant for the next generation coming through.

Looking Forward

I'll be wearing Sync as I prepare for the Sunshine Tour and throughout the 2026 season. It's great to have kit I can rely on when it matters most.

If you want to check them out, head over to syncequestrian.com. They ship internationally too!

— Jasmine 🇬🇧🐴👧 x
